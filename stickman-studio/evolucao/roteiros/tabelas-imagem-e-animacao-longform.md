# Tabelas Imagem + Animação — Long-form

Cada tópico com o prompt de IMAGEM (gerar o frame) e o de ANIMAR (image-to-video).
Colar a trava antes do prompt de imagem. Anderson SEM orelhas.

## Trava (antes do prompt de IMAGEM)
> [CHAR] Flat 2D vector cartoon stickman "Anderson": round white head, spiky black hair,
> stubble beard, small round eyes, thick eyebrows, no ears, navy suit, cream shirt, navy
> tie, black shoes, thick black outline. Clean flat vector, minimalist, solid pastel
> background, soft shadow. horizontal 16:9, no text.

Regra de animação: keep the flat 2D style and Anderson (no ears) stable, no morphing,
smooth looping cartoon motion ~4–6s.

## Extras
- Thumbnails (Estilo A): trocar "clean flat vector…" por "bold comic sticker-art, neon accents, flying money, red crashing chart, dramatic comic lighting" + espaço à esquerda pro título.
- Intro 7 Mentiras: Anderson pointing at his glowing brain while money slips from his pocket → animar: brain pulses, coins drift out, "gotcha" look.
- Surtos (Armadilhas 3/6/9/12): no clipe animado, adicionar zoom rápido + tremor de câmera.
