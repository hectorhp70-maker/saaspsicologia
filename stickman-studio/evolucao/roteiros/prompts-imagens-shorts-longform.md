# Prompts de Imagem — Shorts & Long-form

Para gerar imagens (capas, frames, thumbnails) no Meta AI, ChatGPT, Leonardo, etc,
mantendo a estética e os detalhes do Anderson idênticos em qualquer IA.

## 🔒 BÍBLIA ESTÉTICA (colar SEMPRE no começo do prompt)

### Character lock — Anderson (não muda nunca)
> Flat 2D vector cartoon stickman mascot named "Anderson": round white head, short
> spiky black hair, light stubble beard, small round eyes, thick eyebrows, big round
> ears, wearing a navy-blue business suit with a cream shirt, navy tie, navy trousers
> and black shoes, thick clean black outline.

### Estilo A — CAPA / THUMBNAIL (vibrante, tipo o avatar)
> Bold comic sticker-art, thick white outline around the character, cel-shaded, dark
> blue-to-purple cracked background with glowing neon accents, flying green dollar
> bills, golden "$" coins, glowing purple and pink neon dollar signs, a red crashing
> chart arrow, dynamic action lines, dramatic comic lighting, high contrast, no text.

### Estilo B — FRAME DENTRO DO VÍDEO (flat, limpo)
> Clean flat vector illustration, minimalist, solid pastel background, soft ground
> shadow, simple scenario props, smooth cartoon look, no text, no letters.

**Regra:** [BÍBLIA] + [Estilo A ou B] + [cena-chave abaixo] + [formato]
Formato Shorts = `vertical 9:16`. Formato Long-form = `horizontal 16:9`.
Sempre terminar com **"no text, no letters"** (as IAs erram texto; escreva depois).
